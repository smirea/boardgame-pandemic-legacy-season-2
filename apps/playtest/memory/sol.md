## Rules that change play

Starting a turn in an infested city exposes you. A shelter stops that. Baghdad and Tripoli shelters are permanent. Baghdad is infested. Tripoli is not. A haven can be sheltered. The Tripoli key still demanded a matching card.

When infection removes a cube, anyone may discard a card of that colour to keep it. Act on the first look. Checking the city first loses the race.

Broker discards a city card and takes one of the same colour from the discard. Helmsman makes the first Drive or Ferry each turn free. Radio Operator transfer reaches any seat and is free.

System Produce did not fill Jakarta or the later havens, and it emptied the stockpile. Do not count on it for a new haven or Jakarta.

Inoculate of an infection card is free and stores it. With no Epidemic it stayed out. An Epidemic removes 1 cube, not every cube, and strikes a stored card first.

A forsaken city is skipped and another card is flipped, so the hit still lands. St. Petersburg is forsaken, infested, population 0. Restore would bring it to 1 and leave it infested. It was not restored.
