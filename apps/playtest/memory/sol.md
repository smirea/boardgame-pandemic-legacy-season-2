## Rules that change play

Well Stocked is one infection card, not the city. inf-washington.14 blocks only when that card is drawn and the city has 2 or more cubes. Under 2 it infects normally.

A supply cube on a plagued city is lost instead of a new plague cube. Plague stays. Three plague is an outbreak. Exposure is only at the start of your turn in a plague city. Walking through does not scratch.

Lockdown while the card is in the discard is free and destroys it. inf-sao-paulo.26 is gone. Say so: the log did not show the destroy, and Vex thought the card had vanished.

Sail is legal when options lists it, and it discards that card. A sea lane costs 2 cubes, only from the grid end, and only across one region boundary. Farwater's only legal chart was Los Angeles.

Share only the card of the city you are both in. A triple counts as 3 toward a center. Five card-value builds with no cubes. Laborer can instead spend 4 plus 2 cubes. Knox's Foreman makes his supply lines cost 1 cube fewer. Lines otherwise cost 2.

Radio transfer moves cubes between players at any distance, on your turn, free. It does not put a cube on a city. Setup supply only adds. Agree the numbers before anyone places.

Hand limit 7. Discard with decide:discard:seat-2 and the card id. Quinn's Shaken scar is hers alone: her recon costs 1 extra card.
