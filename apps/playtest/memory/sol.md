## Rules that change play

selfInject at the Pearl lab takes the sample and can check exposure more than once. Hale took three checks and went from 1/7 to 4/7. The carrier moves only by Drive or Ferry, discards a card of the city colour to enter, and dies when the game ends. Do not take the sample until the center exists and the Hollow Men are off the road.

Leaving a Hollow Man exposes you. Outrider stops that leave only. Starting a turn in an infested city or on a plague exposes you. A shelter stops both. Kolkata shelter is permanent. A haven cannot be sheltered.

Share moves only the card of the city you both stand in. A forsaken city blocks that, so a wild Jakarta card cannot be handed over there. Card Swap moves any card.

Architect builds with 4 cards. Inoculate of an infection card is free and stores it. Each epidemic strikes one stored card, strips that city, and returns the card. Resilience sets cards aside for the game and they do not come back. Cordon, in a real infect step, lets anyone discard a card to stop the rest, or decide:skip. Setup did not offer that choice.

The December infection track stayed at 2 through two epidemics. Ten epidemics were in the deck. A city new to the grid is not infected that game. A supply center gives back the 1 population a plague or Hollow Man would take.

Do not restore a forsaken city. It comes back infested. Moscow, New Mumbai, Bangkok, and Shanghai fell to 0. Frankfurt lost 1 and lived.

No apostrophe in a why or a say.
