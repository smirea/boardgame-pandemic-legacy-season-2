## Rules that change play

- Win needs both starred goals. October is 3 new centers and one Hollow Man in each of 3 havens at the same time.
- Broadcast: stand in a tower city, move one Hollow Man from a city of that colour into a neighbour. One action. A figure already in a haven cannot be broadcast out. Black only reaches Midsea. Yellow only reaches Southreach (São Paulo or Lagos). Northwatch only from New York or London. Frankfurt only touches Istanbul.
- Tower costs 2 cubes from you. It transmits its colour at any distance, including giving a card of that colour to another seat.
- Leaving Hollow Men exposes you. Starting a turn on them does not, if there is no plague. Starting on plague does. A shelter stops both the leave and the game-end population loss. Outrider stops only the leave.
- A haven with a Hollow Man and no shelter loses 1 population. A supply center cancels that loss (London and Frankfurt did).
- Last 3 setup infections place Hollow Men. Later, a gather card makes the next city get a Hollow Man instead of an infection.
- An epidemic strips a stored infection card's city, then that card is back in the deck. Cubes do not survive the strip. The follow-up infect plagues a bare city. Do not inoculate a city we cannot afford to plague.
- At 7 incidents, one more hit loses. Before any epidemic, cube every bare city whose card is in the discard. Riyadh at 3 plague did not outbreak on the cube that made it 3. Treat the next hit as the outbreak until that is proved.
- Well Stocked blocks only while 2 or more cubes remain. `washington.8` is triple for builds. `london.32` is double. `lagos.57` is yellow and also red.
- Hand limit without Record Keeper is 7. Rationing 2: take Quiet Night. It is the card that skips the infect that killed us. Ask before the second pick. Scavenge is still unplayed and rationed.
- Safe Houses is destroyed. It placed only 1 shelter, not 3. Do not count on that card.
- Permanent center cap is full at 6. Lagos and Frankfurt were built and will be gone. Do not buy a restore. Forsaken stays infested, and only Drive or Ferry works there.
