## Rules that change how I play

Laborer builds with 4 cards of the city's color plus 2 cubes on me, in that city. The key ends in `:true`. Duplicates count. Both builds put the 2 cubes back in the stockpile. The city cubes did not change. establishLine did the same. Hold the cubes when the key asks, take them from the stockpile, and do not strip a city that is still in the deck.

establishLine links a revealed neighbor. The cube cost is not on the key. Linking a city already on the grid does not add it to the infection deck. Linking Atlanta, Denver, San Francisco, Los Angeles, or Mexico City does. Do not.

Recon is only for a region that is still closed, in that city's own supply center. Washington kept saying "RECON here: 3 blue cards, needs a supply center" after the center was built and after North America was already open. I stood there with 4 blues. Flint stood there with exactly 3. Neither got the action. Lagos wants 5 different yellow cities and only Jacksonville, Sao Paulo, and Lagos exist. Cairo wants 4 different black cities and only Istanbul, Tripoli, and Cairo exist. The recons that can still be real are Sao Paulo (4 yellow, center in Sao Paulo) and London (4 blues of different cities, center in London). A center anywhere else does not unlock them. Try the action the turn the cards and the center are in the same place. If it is missing, stop spending blues there.

Search is listed only on a card that shows `(N search box)`, and only in that city. `search:<card>:0`. Chicago's box put 2 stockpile cubes on the city and the label cleared. The card stayed a normal city card.

Quiet Night skips the next infect when played before the first flip. During the action step it skipped that turn's infect. It does not cube the stripped city.

Supply Drop puts 4 reserve cubes on one grid city. That is the epidemic play: after the strip, before the flip. Purge removes every plague cube and adds no supply. It was in the player deck, not in the rationed reserve. Resilience sets aside up to 2 discarded infection cards of one city. Drawn as the card after the epidemic, the discard was already empty and it would not play.

Airlift moves a pawn and does not deliver. Radio transfer moves cubes between characters at any distance and does not put them on a city. Administrator moves one pawn onto another, once a turn, as an action. Do not end a turn, and do not get pulled, onto a plague city. Exposure is the start of the turn. I was on Tripoli when it plagued and the game ended first. Exposure stayed 0/7.

The epidemic strip leaves the city at 0, then the infect uses the new rate. The track was three 2s, two 3s, then 4. 100% means the pile has 2 cards and still holds the epidemic. No action fits between the strip and the flip.

Share only the card of the city you are both in. Holt held Card Swap all game. Sol said it would not trade a yellow, only a New York, an Istanbul, and my Tripoli. I never saw the keys. Do not plan on it.

Produce works in a haven or a supply center. Local fills that place to population, and the card hit the discard still showing 2 uses. System fills every haven and center. That card hit the discard showing 1 use left. Havens are population 3 and never infect. Do not count on a second use staying in hand.

Hand limit is 7. Discard with `decide:discard:seat-3:<card>` the moment it says so.

Cubes can sit above population. Produce stops at population. A 0-cube city in the top of the infection deck is the one to walk a cube to. Rate 4 filled the track in one infect.

A loss keeps revealed regions, grid links, and population changes. It did not keep centers last time. Look before assuming Washington, Lagos, or Chicago still have one. This loss: Jacksonville -1, London -1, Tripoli -1, Lagos +1, Chicago +1.
