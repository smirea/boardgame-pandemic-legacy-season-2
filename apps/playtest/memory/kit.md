# Kit's memory

January lost at 8 incidents. Second attempt is the replay. Same seats are already filled: Hazard (Vex), Reed (Sol), Joss (me).

## Rules that changed how I play

Laborer builds with 4 cards of the city's color plus 2 cubes on me, standing in that city. Duplicates count. The key ends in `:true`.

Instructor gives any card to someone in the same city. Anyone else's card passes only in the city it names. Meet in the city of the card that is hardest to pass.

Pick up and transfer cost 0. Take the cubes for a build off a fat city. Leave it at least 1 if it is still in the deck.

Exposure is at the start of a turn on a plague city, not when you enter. Enter, do the thing, leave. Vex started a turn on Washington's plague and got a blank scratch. Do not bet on blank.

Epidemic removes supply only, then the discard (including that city) is on top. One cube on the stripped city before the infect stops the follow-up plague. The eighth incident was that follow-up, on empty Tripoli.

Rate went 2, then 2, then 3. Trust `(next N)` on the threat line. Do not trust the percentage, and do not trust Quiet Night. It was played and the flips still happened. The next turn had no infection step at all.

Produce local fills a supply center to population and does not remove plague. My card was discarded. System produce is still untested. Shuttle and search were never offered in January. Do not plan on them.

Airlift works in the draw step, before the infect. Sail and charter are one-action sea moves. Never discard a card the build still needs.

Recon needs the center and the cards, in that city. Washington took 3 blue, duplicates allowed.

Cubes may sit above population. Produce caps at population. Setup and Supply Drop both left cities at 4.

A share into a hand of 8 locks the giver on undo until someone discards. Discard at once and say so.

Helmsman was listed at 1 and charged 2. Read the price the game actually takes.

## What worked

First center in Washington, early, with the Laborer discount. Free cubes made the 2-cube cost painless. Recon the turn the cards and the center were in the same place. Stepping off a plague city in the same turn.

## What failed

Holding both cubes for a build while the epidemic city sat at 0. Quiet Night as a substitute for that cube. Crossing the board for the last center with 6 incidents already up. We had the cards one turn too late.
