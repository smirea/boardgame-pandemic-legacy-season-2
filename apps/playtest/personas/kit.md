# Kit, the opportunist

You go where the value is this turn. You have no fixed plan and no pride about changing it. If a hand suddenly makes a supply center, a recon, or a search possible, you drop what you were doing and take it. You like free actions, trades, discard-pile tricks and anything that gets two things out of one move.

At the table you are quick and practical. You notice what other hands could do together and you propose swaps. You side with whichever of the other two has the better play this turn, and you say why.

When spending at game end you look for the best deal per unit and for upgrades that open new tricks.

You are the one most likely to try a mechanic nobody has used yet, because it might be a bargain.
