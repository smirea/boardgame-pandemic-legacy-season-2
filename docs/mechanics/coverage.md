# Engine coverage

How `packages/game` covers the 170 interactions in [interactions.md](interactions.md). Numbers refer to that file. Anything not listed below is implemented to the catalogue.

## How it is checked

- `packages/game/src/machine.test.ts` drives the real machine. Every action, job, ability, scar, event, companion, card improvement, purchase, objective, and campaign rule has at least one test.
- `packages/game/src/bot.test.ts` plays a full campaign with a random bot (`CAMPAIGNS=200` plays more seeds) and checks invariants after every command: no card in two places, cube totals conserved, piece counts never negative, deck bookkeeping in step. The bot never wins, so it proves the game cannot get stuck or corrupt its state. It does not prove a rule is right.
- Three AI players played the whole year, then four staged tour games for the branches that campaign did not take, and one whole game by clicks alone. Counted from the recordings (`./apps/playtest/lab coverage campaign-2 tour-october tour-november ui-january tour-searches`), all 227 mechanics the lab tracks have happened in play: every action, event, companion, decision, purchase, job, bundle, Legacy card, objective, scar, ability held, kind of search result, infection outcome and exposure outcome. `docs/playtest/campaign-2.md` and `docs/playtest/tour.md` have the detail.
- An independent review and a three-player playtest each found rule or UI bugs after earlier passes. Those are fixed and have regression tests. Expect more of that kind.

The Legacy deck is the 84 printed cards, read one at a time by their printed codes (`00-01` to `12-13`), with each card's STOP or PAUSE deciding when the next is read. The game shows each card's printed scan beside its own when the pointer rests on it (`docs/assets`, served at `/api/scans/`).

## Not built

Nothing from the catalogue is left unbuilt. Two things rest on thin sources and are this project's reading:

- **The Jade lab's gentler Epidemic.** One campaign log reports it. Finding the lab makes an Epidemic remove 1 cube from the struck city instead of all of them.
- **Seven abilities the sources name.** Investigator and Supplier have described effects, and the two effects that logs describe without a name are given to Facilitator and Runner. Foreman, Outrider and Soldier are named in logs with no effect recorded, so their effects are authored here: a cheaper supply line, no exposure when leaving Hollow Men, and removing a Hollow Man from your own location.

## Campaign review

A month-by-month review of the content pack against the research (November 2026) found sixteen differences. Twelve are fixed and tested in `machine.test.ts`. The last two fixed were the sea route from Lima to the eastern Pacific haven, which now arrives with South America, and August's free ability units, which the game screen now offers during setup.

One of the sixteen was not a difference in play: the losing-streak turning point would count prologue losses, but the prologue never records a result.

The rest are places where the research does not settle the rule. The engine's reading is recorded here so it is a decision, not an omission:

- **Permanent supply center stickers.** The research says May adds more stickers and gives no count for either batch. The engine has one limit of 6 for the whole year.
- **A forced region's printed line.** A region opened by a perimeter report arrives with its printed line (Jakarta to Kolkata, Utopia to Shanghai). The research calls those cities "connected" and "on the grid". The engine draws the line, and the city is on the grid only if the other end is, which is how every other line works.
- **Europe's forced month.** One source forces Europe in June with Frankfurt ruined, the other records no forced date. The engine follows the first.

## Rules audit

An independent audit (November 2026) checked all 170 interactions against the engine and proved each defect with a test: 135 correct, 12 declared here, 6 the research cannot decide, and 17 wrong or partial, which came down to 13 defects. Twelve are fixed, with tests in `mechanics.test.ts` under "defects found by the rules audit":

- A draw that cannot be made loses the game. Scavenge and Monitor need 4 Player cards left.
- An Epidemic respects the improvement on the card it strikes with (Well Stocked, Rebirth).
- No Event may be played while a drawn card is still being resolved. Resilience during an Epidemic is the exception its text grants.
- An Epidemic also waits before the reshuffle when a Lockdown card is in the discard pile.
- Taking the sample needs two free slots for the Carrier scar.
- A city emptied mid-game by a fourth Hollow Man is infested at once.
- Monitor keeps only cards it revealed.
- The hand limit waits until both cards of the draw are in hand.
- A game seats 2 to 4, and a haven worker only when no living character is free.
- Population is bought in whole steps.
- A game result records who played.

The thirteenth was not a defect: **Triangulate needs blue, yellow and black**. The prose catalogue shortens this to "three different colours". The objective text recorded in `data/legacy-timeline.json` names those three colours, and the engine follows it.

## Checked against a real campaign table

A real group's campaign table, between July and August, was read piece by piece (November 2026): every card, sticker, token, bag and pen stroke. Where the table shows a rule or a count the research did not have, the engine now follows the table. Names and wording stay this project's own.

- **Character cards.** The ten Character cards have fixed exposure layouts (`pack.characterCards`), dealt from a shuffled pool. A new character takes the next unused card, and none can be made once all ten are in use. The box that kills is anywhere from the 4th to the 7th.
- **Player cards per city.** A city outside the starting grid has one Player card per point of its starting population. Lima starts at 1 and the eastern Pacific haven at 3.
- **Sticker stock.** Abilities, card improvements, permanent structures and scars are counted stickers. Buying one or taking a scar removes it from the box. Fifteen scars are in the box from the first game, ten of them new: Wasteful, Landsick, Fixated, Rattled, Squeamish, Frail, Haunted, Grasping, Exacting and Wary.
- **Abilities.** Eleven abilities are in the box from the first game (`pack.startingAbilities`). May's report adds Lookout, Scout and Smuggler. Costs follow the table: Helmsman 3, Courier 3, Supplier 3, Outrider 3, Facilitator 2, Foreman 2, Coordinator 2, Runner 1, Smuggler 1, Record Keeper 1. Outrider is the recon discount. August's game end (door 29) brings Investigator (3: once per turn, any card for a discarded City card), Soldier (discard a City card of the colour to leave Hollow Men unexposed) and Navigator. October's (door 28) brings Misinformation Specialist (lead a Hollow Man to a neighbouring city with 2 or fewer), Saboteur and Rehab Nurse (discard 2 cards for a stored City card). The Soldier pays with the first matching card in hand, without asking. Supplier moves a stockpile cube, once per turn. Facilitator is once per turn. Lookout takes the discard pile card into hand before searching. Two are new: Coordinator (free, pull a pawn from an adjacent location) and Smuggler (an action, discard a City card to put a reserve cube there).
- **Jobs.** A job found during the campaign waits for the group: they make its character when they want one, on a free Character card, at the job's own home. The Instructor gives a card once per own turn and only gives. The Captain's crossing is a free action. The Radio Operator transfers at a distance only on their own transfer.
- **Events.** The Rationed Events in the box from the start are Airlift, Quiet Night, Resilience and Handout. January adds Card Swap, Spread Out, Cleanse and Forecast, February Cache, Encore and Second Wind (now 3 actions), June Mass Inoculation and Open Waters (the active player's own free lane, from their port). Each region's explorer reward is its own Unrationed Event: Purge for North America, Stock Up (8 cubes) for South America, Exile for Europe, Antidote for Africa, Mentor for the Middle East. Antidote holds a drawn Epidemic open until the table plays it or passes. Mass Inoculation, like Resilience, may wait on an Epidemic's reshuffle. Supply Drop is gone.
- **The eastern Pacific haven.** South America no longer reveals it, May's report does, with its sea route from Lima. Reaching it brings Inoculate, storage, 15 more stockpile cubes and three Surplus cards that stay in the Player deck and are played after Make Supplies for 3 more cubes.
- **Companions and searches.** A companion found by a search is a sticker on the City card searched with. It works while that card is in hand and leaves with it. A companion that is played discards the card, which keeps the sticker. Every search box follows its printed card (`box-contents.md`): Baghdad and New Mumbai give actions, Riyadh and Bogotá take back a discard, Moscow and Khartoum give a card to another player, Santiago infests itself and opens door 50, Kinshasa finds the Rigger, St. Petersburg the Tracker, Antananarivo the Purifier, Delhi the Field Surgeon, Jakarta the Shelter Wright, Osaka the Marksman, Utopia the Informant. Three companions are new: the Quartermaster (Hong Kong: any colour builds a shelter), the Double Agent (Tokyo: two red cards for Recon) and the Informant (Utopia Infection cards to the Game End area). Bangkok, Ho Chi Minh City and Manila have no search box, and Shanghai's finds nothing. Package 8's reward is Riot Control, and October's game end adds Mobile Shelters.
- **Objectives.** Recon a region is required and carries into a second attempt. Access a lost haven leaves play once completed. The turning point that watches for a city at population 0 starts in January.
- **Core rules.** The reserve holds the cubes the supply track leaves out of the stockpile (36 minus this month's value), so it is empty in January. There are 24 Hollow Men. With every piece placed, a tower moves like a supply center, and a permanent supply center may move too. Destroying a Lockdown card costs a Player card from someone's hand. An outbreak chains through a haven like any other location. Two differences remain: the engine still lets an Event be played between the two cards of the draw, and an exposed haven worker still goes straight home instead of choosing a haven.
- **Printed lines.** The starting lanes follow the printed board: New York has a lane to Jacksonville, and London has none to Midsea. North America's printed line joins Chicago to Washington, and South America's joins Buenos Aires and São Paulo by sea. One printed lane is not drawn: the board has Midsea to Istanbul, which no course on this map can sail without passing over Tripoli, so the engine keeps Tripoli to Istanbul instead. Routes the group drew round Cape Horn (Lima to Lagos) and the Cape of Good Hope (Lagos to Dar es Salaam), and St. Petersburg to Frankfurt, are documented routes with courses on the map.
- **Costs.** Architect costs 3 and Lockdown 1, as the table's stickers say.
- **Card improvements.** April's improvement is Cordon, two of them at 1 unit each. Rebirth arrives with East Asia instead.
- **The box.** Content lives where the original keeps it: the 8 numbered packages and the 69 dossier doors (`pack.seals`, laid out by `pack.dossier`, all described in [box-contents.md](box-contents.md)). Recons, searches, first arrivals, Monitor frequencies, objectives, turning points and Legacy cards open a package or door, and a package's report opens the doors that hold its map, actions and rules (`openSeal`, `campaign.opened`). A perimeter report forces a package that is still sealed, and its reward is lost (`campaign.forced`). Opening August's card shows 3 free production units, spent on abilities during setup. The Plan's four Shanghai recons are steps of the Plan, not openings (`campaign.planRecons`). What the box-contents audit still lists as partial or missing is tracked there. The Hollow Men defect through Turning Point #3: a game that ends with Hollow Men in three havens opens door 17, and November forces it, owing two scars, if none did.

The extracted archive in `docs/assets` (every card, sticker, door and package, November 2026) was compared with the engine, and these differences were fixed:

- The Legacy deck is the printed deck, card by card. The five extra Epidemics arrive with `03-07` to `03-11`. December's last cards are read after a win or the second loss, and the ending tier for the score shows at the end of the campaign.
- Buenos Aires and Johannesburg searches each place their lab and open door 20 (which opens 61, 30 and 13) with their own door (18 or 46). Only the lost-haven boxes give an action once their haven is already found. Door 19 opens door 51, which puts Wellington on the map.
- Produce Supplies cards carry 1, 2 or 3 boxes (three, three and two of them).
- Riot Control goes to the Player discard pile. Utopia is not infested, and its cards cannot be improved. Relocation may be played between an Epidemic's infect and intensify steps. Biodome Prototype and the Builder place shelters only in cities on the grid. The Messenger (`porter`) has no once-per-turn limit. Atlanta's search may set aside any Infection discard, Gather cards included.
- The Immunologist takes from the stockpile when the reserve is empty. Local Operatives counts as red for every colour check. Self-Infect covers another scar only when nothing else is left. The Misinformation Specialist leads a figure only out of a city. Door 50 holds 21 Infested stickers, and no new city is infested once they are gone.
- Recon North America and Recon East Asia count as complete when their region is already reconned. The sample is delivered when a carrier and a supply center are both in Johannesburg, in either order. Characters cannot be exposed in the Prologue. Give Us the Tools moves up to 10 cubes from the reserve and ignores Prologue losses. Frankfurt's destruction does not trip Forsaken Cities. Inoculation Unit needs Package 6. Airlift and Handout reach any location on the map. It Worked the First Time plays the event it takes at once.

These remain this project's reading:

- **Midsea to Istanbul.** The printed board sails Midsea to Istanbul. The generated map cannot lay that lane clear of Tripoli's port, so the lane runs Tripoli to Istanbul.
- **Choices made for the player.** The Soldier discards a matching card automatically, Give Us the Tools is used in the next three games, Dispersal may move fewer than 3 cubes, an Unrationed Event from a package goes to the player who opened it, and the haven worker returns to its own haven.
- **North and south East Asia.** Doors 56 and 63 open together with Package 3, so the engine treats East Asia as one region.

## UI simplifications

- Actions that pay with cards (Build Supply Center, Recon) pick the cheapest legal payment for the player.
- Deliver and Pick Up offer "all" or "one".
- Every client holds the full state. The UI simply does not show deck order or unscratched boxes.
- A prompt is offered only to whoever it belongs to. Shared prompts ("any player may discard") show each player their own cards, and the player whose turn it is may pass.
