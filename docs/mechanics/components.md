## K-3. Board and graph [R]

### K-3.1 Starting graph [R]

- 3 starting havens, named by the players (North Atlantic, Mediterranean, South Atlantic). All are ports at starting population **3**.
- 9 starting cities, all ports at starting population **3**: New York, Washington, Jacksonville (blue/blue/yellow), São Paulo, Lagos (yellow), London (blue), Istanbul, Tripoli, Cairo (black).
- 12 starting locations in total, all on the grid.
- No supply lines exist. Every starting edge is one of **17** printed sea lanes (exact list in [board.json](data/board.json) and [docs/assets/board/board.json](../assets/board/board.json)).
- Starting city populations are all **3** (sum 27, matching the 27 starting Infection cards).

## K-6. Deck composition [R]

### K-6.1 Player deck [R]

Built every game from:

- City cards for every city on the grid, minus destroyed cards and cards stored in Package 6. Maximum possible across the full box: 36 starting + 47 expansion = **83** City cards (44 cities have cards; Wellington and Lake Baikal have 0).
- Produce Supplies cards still intact (8 at the start).
- Portable Antiviral Lab cards (3, once Package 6 is opened).
- Rationed Events chosen up to the rationing level (0 to 10, chosen from up to 15 unlocked).
- Every Unrationed Event still intact in the reserve (does not count against rationing).
- Companion cards stay on their City cards (so they are already part of the City card pool).
- Epidemics: 5 at the start, then by Door `E` (_Stretched Too Thin_, 5–10 Epidemics).

### K-6.2 Infection deck [R]

Built every game from:

- Infection cards for every city on the grid, minus destroyed cards and cards stored in Package 6. Maximum possible: 27 starting + 54 expansion (including 8 Utopia) = **81** City Infection cards.
- Hollow Men Gather cards (4 from Package 2, +4 from Package 3, +4 from Package 8 = up to 12), staged in the **Infection discard pile** at setup (Rule C / Area 16).

### K-6.3 Package 6 [R]

Two persistent piles stored inside the Package 6 box, each capped at **30**: stored Player cards and stored Infection cards.

## K-7. Cube pools [R]

| Pool          | Rule                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Box total     | 36 starting Supply cubes + 15 new Supply cubes in Package 6 (**51 total**); 8 Plague cubes                                                                                      |
| Reserve       | Supply cubes not in this game's stockpile. Some effects (Immunologist, search results, Turning Point #2, Door 11, Strategic Reserves, Hidden Stockpile, Smuggler) draw from it. |
| Stockpile     | Set at setup from the supply track plus Area 10/11 bonuses (Rule B). Make Supplies and Produce Supplies draw from it.                                                           |
| On the board  | Any number per location                                                                                                                                                         |
| On characters | Any number per character (capped at 3 with the _Weak_ scar)                                                                                                                     |

Plague cubes are limited to 8 by the Incident track, so they never run out before the loss.
