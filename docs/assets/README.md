## Known Publisher Misprints & Archival Notes

- **Port compass icon misprints on 3 Player cards:** The board map stickers are authoritative for port status (white anchor = port, white `×` = inland). On three printed Player cards, the top-right compass icon disagrees with the map sticker:
  - **Hong Kong (`P3-12b`)** & **Shanghai (`P3-14b`)**: Board map sticker (Door `56`) shows a port anchor (`port: true`), while the Player cards print a solid dot without an anchor.
  - **Utopia (`P8-03b`)**: Board map sticker (`package-8/stickers/sticker-01.jpg`) shows an inland white `×` (`port: false`), while the Player card prints a port anchor (and longitude `111°31'60"E`).
- **Four unused Haven Leadership Summit keepsake doors:** Doors `Z` (Folder 1, Summit 4), `26` (Folder 2, Summit 14 — _"All Together!"_), `40` (Folder 3, Summit 45 — _"The Power of Three!"_), and `54` (Folder 4, Summit 70 — _"Let's Go!"_) contain decorative keepsake stickers and are not referenced by any card, package, or door in the box.
- **No "Hollow Men Assemble" cards and no "Surveyor" ability:** Earlier community summaries speculated about December "Assemble" cards and a "Surveyor" ability. Direct inspection of the complete Workshop box confirms neither exists: Package 2, Package 3, and Package 8 each contain 4 _Hollow Men Gather_ cards (12 total), and _Soldier_ (Door `29`) is the ability that prevents Hollow Men exit exposure (by discarding a City card matching the color of the city left).
